Punch-Cutting & Proportional Canons

Deconstruct 16th-century steel punches using high-resolution macro photography and micro-caliper measurements. Rebuild Renaissance grid proportions using modern parametric equations.

  • 1.1 Steel tempering & punch resilience geometry
  • 1.2 Counter-punch depth & ink trap mechanics
  • 1.3 The Tschichold golden canon vs Renaissance margins
READING REQUIREMENT: 2 TEXTBOOKS

Phototypesetting Grid Mathematics

Analyze the mid-century transition from lead to film disc projection. Mathematical modeling of lens distortion, exposure bleeding, and optical scaling charts.

  • 2.1 Film disc rotational velocity & flash timing
  • 2.2 Optical kerning templates in analog optics
  • 2.3 Early rasterization algorithms (1965-1978)
LAB REQUIREMENT: C++ / RUST

Bezier Vector Engine Optimization

Write a bare-metal rasterizer capable of parsing OpenType CFF tables. Optimize cubic-to-quadratic curve conversions for sub-pixel rendering pipelines.

  • 3.1 Cubic Bezier evaluation & De Casteljau subdivision
  • 3.2 Anti-aliasing via analytical area integration
  • 3.3 GPU-accelerated glyph atlas generation
